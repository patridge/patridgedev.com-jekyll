You found the privacy policy for EmojiGames, PatridgeDev's collection of emoji-powered games, including [EmojiPuncher](/emojigames/puncher/). This single policy covers every game published under the EmojiGames name.

## Types of Information Collected

Currently...none. EmojiGames apps do not collect any personal information at this time.

Future updates may collect non-personal usage and technical data to help improve gameplay and performance.

## How Collected Data is Used

Since nothing is collected at this point, no data is available for use.

When non-personal usage and technical data is collected, it will be used for improving the experience for future versions of EmojiGames apps and future PatridgeDev software. Some non-personal data may be used for educational presentations, demos, and blog posts.

## Social Interactions

EmojiGames apps do not currently include social or chat features that might expose you to in-app content from other players.

## Changes to this Policy

As this policy changes, it will be updated here. Please periodically check this privacy policy page for any changes.

## _Notice to California Residents_

Apparently, residents of California are permitted to request information about the disclosure of their personal information when they use EmojiGames apps in the state of California. Since EmojiGames apps don't collect any personal information, such requests will receive an email reiterating this sentence.
