This is the official support page for EmojiGames, PatridgeDev's collection of small, emoji-powered games, including [EmojiPuncher](/emojigames/puncher/).

## Contact

For help with an app issue, general feedback, or a feature request, email [support@patridgedev.com](mailto:support@patridgedev.com?subject=EmojiGames%20Support).

Please include the following details when they are relevant:

* The game name and version
* Your device model and iOS version
* What you expected to happen and what happened instead
* Steps that reproduce the issue
* A screenshot or screen recording, if it helps explain the problem

You can generally expect a response within 5–7 business days.

## Troubleshooting

Before contacting support, try these common fixes:

1. Update the game to the latest version available to you.
2. Update your device to the latest compatible version of iOS.
3. Close and reopen the game.
4. Restart your device.

If the problem continues, contact support with the details above.

## EmojiPuncher

### How do I control my fighter?

Tap to launch your fighter, or drag and fling its head. Its arms follow with physics-driven momentum and damage enemies when they collide.

### How can I report a crash or gameplay problem?

Email [support@patridgedev.com](mailto:support@patridgedev.com?subject=EmojiPuncher%20Issue) with the troubleshooting details above. If possible, describe which wave you were playing and what happened immediately before the problem.
